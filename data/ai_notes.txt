Artificial Intelligence is a branch of computer science.
AI enables machines to learn from data and make decisions.

Machine Learning is a subset of Artificial Intelligence.
It allows computers to learn patterns from data without being explicitly programmed.

Deep Learning uses neural networks to solve complex problems.
Large Language Models like GPT are based on deep learning.

RAG stands for Retrieval Augmented Generation.
RAG combines document retrieval with large language models.
It helps AI systems answer questions using external knowledge.
